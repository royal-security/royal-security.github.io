# About Royal Security

Royal Security is an independent cybersecurity team focused on ethical hacking, red teaming, blue team defense, CTF competitions, and security research.

## Our Mission

To learn, practice, and grow together in the field of cybersecurity while following strict ethical standards.

## What We Do

- Ethical hacking and penetration testing (on authorized systems only)
- Blue team operations and defense
- Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions
- Security research and vulnerability disclosure
- Training and skill development for beginners

## Our Values

- **Ethics first** – we never attack systems without permission.
- **Continuous learning** – we share knowledge and help each other grow.
- **Teamwork** – we collaborate across departments.
- **Integrity** – we respect privacy and laws.

## Why Join Us?

- Free training and learning paths
- Hands-on labs and CTF practice
- Opportunity to build a real cybersecurity portfolio
- Supportive community
