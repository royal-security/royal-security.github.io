# Departments

## Red Team
Focus: Ethical hacking, penetration testing, red team operations.  
Tools: Kali Linux, Metasploit, Burp Suite, Nmap.

## Blue Team
Focus: Defense, log analysis, incident response.  
Tools: Wireshark, Splunk, Security Onion, Sysmon.

## CTF & Research
Focus: CTF competitions, writeups, vulnerability research.  
Platforms: TryHackMe, HackTheBox, CTFtime.

## OSINT
Focus: Open-source intelligence, reconnaissance.  
Tools: Maltego, theHarvester, Shodan, Google Dorking.

## Training
Focus: New member onboarding, learning paths, certifications.  
Resources: TryHackMe, PortSwigger Academy, OverTheWire.

## Media & Content
Focus: Blog posts, writeups, LinkedIn, social media.
